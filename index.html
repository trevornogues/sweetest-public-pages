What is Sweetest?

A new kind of app for people with opinions about dessert.

No stars. No five-paragraph reviews. Just your personal top-10 of the sweet things you’ve actually eaten: cookies, croissants, ice cream, boba. And a slowly-emerging consensus from everyone in your city.

  • 01
    Personal rankings, one comparison at a time.

    Every category is its own top-10. Add a treat, compare it head-to-head against a few you’ve already ranked, and your list reorders itself. You never type a score.

  • 02
    Friends-only feed, photo first.

    See what the people you trust just ranked, with their notes and photos. No influencers, no algorithm, no strangers in your feed.

  • 03
    City leaderboards and a map.

    Your picks quietly add up into anonymous city, state, and national bests. Open the map to see where the top-ranked spots actually are. It’s okay to disagree.

  • 04
    Pop-ups get their own home.

    Rank bakers without storefronts in a separate Pop-Up category so they don’t get buried. See what’s happening in your city this week and star the ones you want to go to.

  • 05
    Taste Identity and a monthly challenge.

    Tag and slide each entry and see where your taste leans, then compare with a friend. Each month, a featured category: rank five new spots and finish the Sweet Challenge.

  • 06
    Private by default if you want.

    Flip privacy on and only your approved followers see your lists. Your votes still count toward city leaderboards, completely anonymously.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is Sweetest free?

Yes. Sweetest is free on iPhone and in the Android beta. There is no subscription, no in-app purchase, and no advertising.

Can I keep my lists private?

Yes. Set your profile to private and only followers you approve can see your lists, notes, and photos. Your rankings still count toward city leaderboards, anonymously.

How do city leaderboards work?

Every ranking you save adds to an anonymous, aggregated list for your city, state, and country. Nobody can see who ranked what from the leaderboard. Rankings with tags and a slider carry a little more weight, which rewards thoughtful entries. Leaderboards are most alive in Seattle, New York, Los Angeles, and San Francisco; personal lists work anywhere.

How do I get the Android version?

Android is in closed testing on Google Play. Open the testing link, sign in with the Google account you use for the Play Store, and install. If Google says you are not eligible yet, email sweetestbitesapp@gmail.com with that account and I’ll add you.

How do I delete my account?

From Settings inside the app, or by emailing sweetestbitesapp@gmail.com from the address on your account. Deleting removes your profile, lists, photos, comments, and follows. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your account page, and what is stored is in the Privacy Policy.
