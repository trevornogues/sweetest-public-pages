What is Sweetest?

A new kind of app for people with opinions about dessert.

No stars. No five-paragraph reviews. Just your personal top-10 of the sweet things you’ve actually eaten: cookies, croissants, ice cream, boba. And a slowly-emerging consensus from everyone in your city.

  • 01
    Personal rankings.

    Every category is its own top-10. Add a treat, compare it head-to-head against a few you’ve already ranked, and your list reorders itself. You never type a score.

  • 02
    Friends-only feed.

    See what the people you trust just ranked, with their notes and photos. No influencers, no algorithm, no strangers in your feed.

  • 03
    City & region leaderboards.

    Your picks quietly add up into anonymous city-wide bests. Find out whether the place you love is the place everyone else loves too. It’s okay to disagree.

  • 04
    Private by default if you want.

    Flip privacy on and only your approved followers see your lists. Your votes still count toward city leaderboards, completely anonymously.

What you’ll rank

Specific enough to actually matter.

A top-10 for “desserts” means nothing. A top-10 for chocolate chip cookies in your city is a fight worth having. Sweetest ships with the categories below, and you can add your own.

As our data grows, we’ll also surface curated lists that get even more specific: think “the best carrot cake in New York” or “top soft serve in Seattle.”

The pilot

Going deep in four cities first.

Your personal lists work anywhere. But the city leaderboards only feel alive once enough locals have ranked. If you eat sweets in any of these four, we’d especially love your help getting them going.

  • 🏔️ Seattle
  • 🗽 New York
  • 🌴 Los Angeles
  • 🌉 San Francisco

Outside these four? You can still join. Your personal lists work everywhere, but city leaderboards stay quiet until we expand.

Getting in

Three taps to join the beta.

  1. Join the beta through TestFlight on iPhone or a Google Play closed test invite on Android.
  2. If you’re on iPhone, install Apple’s TestFlight app when prompted. If you’re on Android, use the Google Play testing link we send you.
  3. Accept the beta invite, then install Sweetest. The app will appear on your home screen.

Run into trouble? Email noguestj@gmail.com and I’ll help you get in.
