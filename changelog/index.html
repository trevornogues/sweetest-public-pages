Changelog

What shipped, and when. Newest first.

1.10 September 25, 2026

  • Small polish and reliability improvements.
  • You can now rate Sweetest from Settings.

1.9 September 8, 2026

Sweetest is more photo-first, so you can browse desserts by what they look like, not just by name.

  • Rankings and Discover lead with dessert photos, including Spotlight and trending photo tiles.
  • Profiles get a photo cover band, photo grids for Posts, and photo tiles for your lists.
  • Taste views (yours and places’) show real ranking photos as evidence.

1.8 August 8, 2026

Pop-ups get their own home on Sweetest.

  • Rank pop-ups. Log bakers without storefronts in a new Pop-Up category, separate from dessert rankings so they don’t get buried.
  • Upcoming pop-ups. See what’s happening this week in your city from the Feed, browse the full list, and switch cities anytime.
  • Star what you want to go to and see how many others are going too.
  • Explore and Search surface trending pop-ups on their own, so local bakers are easier to find.

1.7 July 28, 2026

  • Monthly Sweet Challenge. Rank 5 new spots in a featured treat category each month. Complete it and celebrate with friends.
  • Map improvements, place profile updates, and Forgot Password on login.

1.6 June 23, 2026

  • Spotlight photos with credits to contributors on location pages. Add a pic to your rankings for a chance to be featured.
  • Removed Group Rankings and replaced it with Maps integration, to make room for what’s next.
  • Onboarded locations. Some spots now have their full menu and custom tags to pre-select from, which makes logging simpler and keeps the data consistent.
  • Tag users in comments. General UI improvements.

1.5 June 18, 2026

  • Maps in the Search tab. See a map of the top rankings in one of the 4 pilot cities and where they are.
  • Improved Taste Profile and Taste Insights.
  • Fixed the category slider bug. Richer set of category tags. Tag users in comments.

1.4 May 29, 2026

  • Maps in the Search tab (first version).
  • Improved Taste Profile and Taste Insights. Updated category icons. General UI fixes.

1.3 May 25, 2026

  • Rankings with at least 2 tags and one slider get a 1.2x weight on community aggregated rankings, to promote higher-quality entries.
  • Taste Profile. Add tags and slider values to entries and see what types of treats you rank most. View friends’ Taste Profiles and how similar you are.
  • Location Taste Profiles when viewing a place.

1.2 May 22, 2026

  • New tagging and slider mechanisms for ranking items. Rankings with at least 3 tags or sliders get a 1.2x weight on community rankings.
  • First version of Taste Profile.
  • Updated ranking flow: type in the place before the item.

1.1 May 18, 2026

  • User leaderboard. Who has the biggest sweet tooth? See where you stand across all Sweetest users.
  • Group rankings to settle a debate with friends. (Replaced by Maps in 1.6.)
  • General UI improvements from user feedback.

1.0 May 15, 2026

First release on the App Store.

  • Personal top-10 rankings by category, built by head-to-head comparison. No star ratings.
  • Custom categories.
  • Friends feed with notes, photos, likes, and comments.
  • City, state, and national leaderboards, anonymous and aggregated.
  • Place search for nearby bakeries, cafes, and dessert spots.
  • Public or private profiles. Reporting, blocking, and account deletion controls.
